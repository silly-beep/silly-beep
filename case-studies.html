Meta Ads · Bangalore

EduVision Academy cuts CPL by 78%

The Problem

A Bengaluru coaching institute spending ₹80K/month on Meta Ads with ₹800+ CPL and poor lead quality — sales team overwhelmed with unqualified leads.

Our Strategy

Rebuilt the funnel with intent-based creative testing, a qualification quiz as the lead form, and geo-fenced targeting around 12 Bangalore neighbourhoods.

The Result

CPL dropped from ₹800 to ₹180 in 90 days, with a 3x improvement in lead-to-enrollment conversion rate.

Web Development · Mysore

Heritage Palace Homestay cuts OTA dependency

The Problem

A heritage homestay near Mysore Palace relied almost entirely on OTA platforms, paying 15–20% commission on every booking with no direct customer relationship.

Our Strategy

Built a booking-optimized website targeting Palace-visit and heritage-stay searches, paired with a Google Ads campaign for high-season demand and email capture for repeat guests.

The Result

Direct bookings up 190% in the first season, materially reducing OTA commission spend while improving guest relationships.
