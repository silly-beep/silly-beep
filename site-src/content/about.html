Our Story

From a 3-person team to Karnataka's growth engine

SYN Media Technology started in 2013 as a three-person team in a small Indiranagar office, helping local Bangalore businesses get their first websites online. What began as basic web design work quickly evolved as clients kept asking the same question: "the website looks great, but how do people actually find us?"

That question became our obsession. By 2016 we'd built a dedicated performance marketing division running Meta and Google Ads. By 2019 we'd crossed 100 active clients across education, real estate, hospitality and e-commerce. In 2022 we started building AI-powered automation into every client's marketing stack — before "AI marketing" was a category most agencies talked about.

In 2024, after years of requests from businesses in Mysuru, we opened a dedicated Mysore studio in Vijayanagar. It wasn't a satellite office — it's a full local team that understands Mysore's mix of heritage tourism, education institutions and a fast-growing D2C scene. Today we serve 250+ brands across both cities with a consistent 4.8x average return on ad spend.
