    Performance marketing FAQs

    What's the minimum ad budget you recommend?
    We recommend a minimum of ₹30,000/month in ad spend to gather enough data for meaningful optimization, though this varies by industry and city competitiveness.
    How is your management fee structured?
    We charge a flat monthly management fee or 15% of ad spend (whichever is higher, minimum ₹20,000/month) — never a percentage that incentivizes us to waste your budget.
    Can you run geo-targeted campaigns for Mysore specifically?
    Yes — we run separate, geo-fenced campaigns for Bangalore and Mysore audiences with location-specific ad copy and landing pages for better conversion rates.
