    Website development FAQs

    How long does a new website take to build?
    A standard 5–10 page business website takes 2–4 weeks from signed brief to launch. Ecommerce and custom web apps typically take 5–8 weeks depending on integrations.
    Do you build on WordPress, Shopify, or custom code?
    All three — we choose the platform based on your needs. WordPress for content-heavy sites your team wants to edit themselves, Shopify for ecommerce, and hand-coded builds for maximum speed and custom functionality.
    Will my website be optimized for local Bangalore or Mysore search?
    Yes — every website we build includes technical SEO foundations and, where relevant, local business schema markup so your site is ready for local SEO from day one.
    Do you offer website maintenance after launch?
    Yes, 30 days of support is included with every build, and ongoing maintenance plans are available for security updates, backups, and content changes.
