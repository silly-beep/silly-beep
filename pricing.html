Pricing FAQs

Is there a minimum contract period?
No — all our retainer plans are month-to-month. We earn your business every month through results, not a lock-in contract.
Does the price differ between Bangalore and Mysore clients?
No, our pricing is the same across both cities. What differs is the strategy — ad budgets and channel mix are tailored to each market's competitiveness.
Is ad spend included in the management fee?
No — ad spend is billed directly by Google/Meta to your account. Our fee covers strategy, creative, and ongoing optimization.
Can I switch plans as my business grows?
Yes, you can move between Starter, Growth and Enterprise at any time — most clients start on Starter or Growth and scale up once they see results.
Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?
We offer a limited number of discounted slots each quarter for early-stage Bangalore and Mysore startups and registered nonprofits — ask us during your consultation.
